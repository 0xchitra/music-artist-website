Where Every Note
Tells a Story

Kaif Tanjilur Rahman is an independent singer from Assam, driven by emotion, nature, and honest storytelling. His music blends Assamese and Hindi, rooted in the warmth of folk tradition and shaped by contemporary production.

From the romantic poetry of Prem Gatha to the devotion in Tumar Namote, each song is a chapter, each verse a feeling he hopes you'll carry with you long after the music ends.
